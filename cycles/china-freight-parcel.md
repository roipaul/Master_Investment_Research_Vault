# China freight and parcel cycle note

Covers [[YMM]] (Full Truck Alliance, the road-freight matching platform) and [[ZTO]] (ZTO Express). Two linked cycles: express parcels, where the readings are volume and revenue per parcel, and road freight, where they are tonnage, the spot rate and the number of trucks chasing loads. The fixed indicators are in `cycles/index.md`. The running evidence log for parcels is [[china-express-delivery-cycle]]. Chinese sources are translated here and cited in the original. Everything is an observation or Claude's read. Nothing is a forecast, and nothing here is Paul's view.

## Phase log

- 2026-11-26: **Parcels: Rolling over. Road freight: Trough, extended** (first cycle note). Claude's read, proposed.

## Read of 2026-11-26 (first build)

### Bottom line

- **Parcels: Rolling over.** National parcel volume fell 0.5% on the year in October, the first monthly fall in the 2026 figures on file, after growing 5.0% in the first half and about 3% in the third quarter. Revenue per parcel is still up about 2.8% on the year, but that gain has narrowed every month since August.
- **Road freight: Trough, extended.** The freight rate index was 104.9 in October, the same as a year earlier. Tonnage growth has slowed to 2.5%, the logistics profit index is at 47, and heavy-truck sales fell 16% in October.
- **Both names are growing faster than their industries.** Full Truck Alliance's orders rose 11.3% in Q3 against road tonnage up about 2.5%. ZTO's parcels rose 7.0% against about 3% for the industry. That is share and penetration, not the cycle, and it is the main disagreement between the names and the indicators.
- **Full Truck Alliance's operating margin (46.9% in Q3) is about 72% above its 2023-2025 average.** The primer puts the rise down to mix, so this may be structural; the vault has no test of it. ZTO could not be sized.
- **Not read:** road freight volume for January to October, October price per parcel for Yunda and SF, and an earnings update for either name.

### 2. Phase

Previous phase: none. No earnings update with a cycle read is on file for either name.

**Parcels: Rolling over.**
- For: volume growth has stepped down each period and went negative in October (row 1). The price recovery that began with the 2025 campaign against below-cost pricing is narrowing as last year's increases are lapped, and YTO is cutting price to take share (row 3). ZTO's own guide was cut in August and its management says slower growth will "become the industry norm" ([[china-express-delivery-cycle]]).
- Against: revenue per parcel is still above a year ago, the three listed couriers' Q3 profits rose 28 to 79%, and the listed names are still growing volume 7 to 12% by taking share. One month of falling national volume is one reading; November includes Singles' Day.

**Road freight: Trough, extended.**
- For: the rate index is flat on the year at a level the federation describes as oversupplied (row 4), profit is contracting (row 5), tonnage growth is slowing (row 6) and truck buyers have pulled back (row 7).
- Against, in the direction of worse: slowing tonnage and a year of heavy truck buying in 2025 mean more capacity against less growth. Against, in the direction of better: falling truck sales are how overcapacity ends, and the federation notes official steps against "involutionary" low-price competition [8].

**The disagreement.** Neither name's results look like its industry's. Full Truck Alliance grew orders 11% and adjusted operating income 98% in a flat freight market; ZTO grew parcels 7% while the industry grew 3%. The cycle note cannot tell Paul how much of either is at risk from the cycle. That is a question for each name's own work: for Full Truck Alliance, whether order growth holds when tonnage growth is 1 to 2% and Q4 revenue is guided flat; for ZTO, whether share gains hold as YTO cuts price.
