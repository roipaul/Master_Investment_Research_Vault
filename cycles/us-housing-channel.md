# US housing channel cycle note

Covers [[POOL]] (Pool Corp), [[FDR-MC]] (Fluidra), [[0669-HK]] (Techtronic) and Pentair (PNR, which has no hub in the vault). The cycle is US housing-linked demand as it reaches distributors and their suppliers: home sales and new building lead the discretionary part (new pools, remodels, big projects), while upkeep of what is already installed does not move with them. The fixed indicators are in `cycles/index.md`. The running evidence log for pools is [[pool-industry-cycle]]. Everything here is an observation or Claude's read. Nothing is a forecast, and nothing here is Paul's view.

## Phase log

- 2026-11-26: **Downturn to Trough** (first cycle note). The names' own Q3 updates read "Trough, extended". The outside housing data were still falling in October, so this note gives a range and does not confirm a bottom. Claude's read, proposed.

## Read of 2026-11-26 (first build)

### Bottom line

- **Phase: Downturn to Trough.** The names say Trough. The data that lead their discretionary sales say it has not stopped falling: existing-home sales in October were the lowest since August 2010, single-family permits the lowest since January 2023, and the mortgage rate is a full point above a year ago.
- **That disagreement is the main finding.** Every pool company plans for a flat 2027. Their plans were set on calls held between 22 and 29 October; the October housing figures came out after that, on 12 and 18 November.
- **What is not falling:** repair and remodel spending is flat to slightly up in dollars, both big-box chains grew comparable sales about 2% on higher tickets, and distributors' inventories are already cut.
- **Earnings against mid-cycle could not be sized for any name.** New-pool volume is about 31% below its ten-year average, but the split of each company's sales between upkeep and discretionary work is not on file, and neither are ten-year margins.
- **Not read:** the direct FRED and Freddie Mac pages (refused to the fetch tool; the original releases were read in their place), year-ago building-materials sales, and anything for Techtronic or Pentair beyond peer comments.

### 2. Phase: Downturn to Trough

Previous phase: none in a cycle note. Both pool names' Q3 earnings updates (2026-11-25) read "Trough, extended".

For Trough (the bottom is in):
- New pools have fallen for five years and sit about 31% below the ten-year average (row 8).
- Channel inventory has already been cut (row 7), so a further fall in sell-through would not be made worse by destocking on the same scale.
- Repair and remodel spending is flat, not falling (rows 4 and 5), and the big-box chains' comparable sales are positive (row 6).
- The aftermarket, which is most of the pool names' sales, is growing on price ([[pool-industry-cycle]]).

For Downturn (still falling):
- The three readings that lead discretionary work all got worse in October: home sales at a 16-year low and down 8.8% on the year, single-family permits down 13%, the mortgage rate a point higher than a year ago (rows 1 to 3).
- The supply of unsold homes is at an 11-year high (row 2) and pending sales, which lead closed sales, were at a record low in September.
- Growth in rows 4 to 6 is price. Home Depot's transactions fell 0.5%; the pool names' volumes are flat to down ([[pool-industry-cycle]]).

**The disagreement.** The names read Trough and plan for a flat 2027. The outside data cannot confirm a trough while the leading series are still falling, and the latest and weakest of those figures were published after the names' Q3 calls. This note does not say that 2027 will be worse than the companies plan. It says the evidence for "flat" is the companies' own, and the evidence against is the housing data.

By part:
- Housing turnover and new building: Downturn.
- Repair and remodel: Trough, flat in dollars.
- New pool construction: Trough, extended, and exposed to the first line.
- Maintenance and replacement: not cyclical in this sense; steady and price-led.
- Channel inventory: destocked.

### 5. What the price assumes

From the names' earnings updates; there is no `MarketRead.md` for any of them.

- [[POOL]] at US$213.85 (2026-11-24): 19.4 times earnings pays for earnings growth of 8% or more a year, which needs the operating leverage management has promised for 2027. It does not need a construction recovery.
- [[FDR-MC]] at EUR 18.55 (2026-11-24): 13.3 times adjusted earnings pays for a flat market and modest margin progress, not for a recovery in construction.
- [[0669-HK]] and Pentair: not on file.

Neither price is paying for a recovery. Neither file tests what a further fall in the discretionary part would do.
