# China offshore-trust individual income tax (MOF/STA Announcement 2026 No. 21; STA No. 15)

- 2026-07-24 — Issued. Chinese tax residents pay 20% IIT when placing assets into an offshore trust (treated as a transfer), annually on trust income whether or not distributed, and on termination. Look-back: placements 2023–2025 and trust income before 2026, to be filed within 90 days (by 2026-10-22) without late fees. Source: Hankun Law commentary (29 Jul 2026); Southern Metropolis Daily via Eastmoney (21 Oct 2026). Original not read.
- Read-across: founders of red-chip companies holding through offshore trusts. Haidilao founders' trust sold 259m shares in Sep 2026 (press inference: to pay tax).
