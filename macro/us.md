# United States: where we stand

A market read: four blocks of fixed indicators, each scored Hot, Neutral or Cold against its own history, after Howard Marks' questions. The Economy block is context. The dial is proposed from the other three. The indicator set is in `cycles/index.md`. This page describes the present. It makes no forecast, takes no view on timing, and is not Paul's view.

## Dial log

- 2026-11-26: first reading. The three dial blocks point to **Neutral**. **No proposal**: a setting is proposed only when two readings in a row point to it, and this is the first. The confirmed dial in `settings.md` is blank and is unchanged.

## Read of 2026-11-26 (first build)

### Bottom line

- **The economy is hot and the price of money is rising.** Growth was 3.4% in Q3 on the first estimate, the Federal Reserve calls inflation "elevated" and held at 3.75-4.00% with two votes to raise, and the 10-year Treasury yields about 5.05%.
- **Credit has cooled in two months.** The high-yield spread went from 2.66% on 21 September to 3.72% on 24 November. The lowest-rated bonds are at 14%, wider than at the April 2025 tariff shock. Investment-grade spreads (0.98%) and the Chicago Fed's conditions index have barely moved.
- **Psychology is split.** Margin debt is 22% above a year ago and near its record, and money is flowing into equity funds, yet the retail survey has been more bearish than average for about 40 weeks and two-thirds of October's new listings trade below issue.
- **The two valuation measures disagree.** On ten-year earnings the market is near 41 times, exceeded only in 2000. On next year's forecast earnings it is 18.3 times, below its ten-year average. The gap is profit margins: 15.0% against a five-year average of 12.6%. The forward figure looks ordinary only if those margins last.
- **Dial arithmetic: Neutral.** Defensive needs two of the three dial blocks Hot. At most one is. One reading changes nothing.
