# Themes

Notes that span companies: industry driver maps, cycle notes, and base-rate files (for example, what happened to earnings after past regulatory interventions). Company hubs link here so that work done for one name is reused for the next.

## Industry and business-model notes (read-across)

The `read-across` skill files reusable learnings here, so that research on one company carries into the next.

- **Industry notes:** `themes/<industry>.md`, one per industry. A company's hub names its note in `industry:`.
- **Business-model notes:** `themes/model-<tag>.md`, one per tag below. They hold what is true of the model wherever it appears.

Each note has one learnings table, appended to and never rewritten:

`L# | Date | Kind | Learning | Applies to | From (company and document) | Original source | Data through | Status`

Kind is fact, benchmark, pattern or open question. Facts, benchmarks and open questions with a primary source are filed automatically. Patterns and lessons are filed as `proposed` and wait for Paul's confirmation.

## Business-model tags

A hub carries one to three of these in `model_tags:`. Companies that share a tag are candidates for comparison; an analogue is used only after Paul confirms it. Add a tag only when none fits, and log the addition below.

| Tag | What it means |
|---|---|
| franchise-network | Earns from franchisees or network partners who own the local assets |
| marketplace | Matches buyers and sellers and takes a fee; owns little of what is traded |
| distributor | Buys, stocks and resells; earns a spread and depends on working capital |
| branded-consumer | Sells its own brands to consumers, directly or through retailers |
| capital-equipment | Makes machines or tools bought on a capex budget; orders lead revenue |
| component-maker | Supplies parts designed into customers' products |
| commodity-producer | Sells an undifferentiated product at a market price; cost position decides |
| lender | Earns a spread on money lent; credit cost and capital decide |
| subscription-software | Recurring fees for software; retention and expansion decide |
| logistics-network | Moves goods through a shared network; density and utilisation decide |
| licensed-operator | Operates under a licence or regulated tariff that limits entry |
| content-and-ip | Earns from owned content or intellectual property across formats |
