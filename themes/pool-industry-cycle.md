# Pool industry cycle

Swimming-pool equipment and distribution: the split between the aftermarket (upkeep and replacement on the installed base) and the discretionary part (new construction and remodel), channel inventory between makers and distributors, price against cost inflation, and the housing and interest-rate data that lead new construction. Bears on [[POOL]] and [[FDR-MC]]; peers read are Pentair, Hayward, Leslie's, Maytronics and Latham, with Home Depot (SRS / Heritage Pool Supply) as a competitor of Pool Corp and a housing bellwether.

Phase as read on 2026-11-25 (Claude's read, proposed): **Trough, extended** for new build and remodel; aftermarket steady and price-led.

## Evidence log

- 2026-11-25 — Industry and peers, read for [[FDR-MC]] and [[POOL]] Q3 2026. **New construction is in its fifth year of decline and nobody plans for a recovery in 2027.** US new in-ground pools: about 55-56 thousand in 2026 (Fluidra's estimate) against just under 60 thousand in 2025 and a ten-year average of 80 thousand, which Pool Corp's new chief executive calls "normalized". Pentair plans 2027 sell-through flat and says its plan does "not anticipate market recovery"; Hayward's call has the industry "coalescing around flat 2027 volumes"; Latham is "planning for a flat year"; Pool Corp: 2027 not "much different" from 2026; Fluidra: "more or less the same situation". The leading data worsened in the autumn: US 30-year mortgage rate 7.33% in October (6.25% a year earlier), existing-home sales at a 3.75m annual rate, down 8.8% on the year (NAR); Home Depot: housing turnover "hasn't moved in 4 years". Call comments are from third-party transcripts.
- 2026-11-25 — **The aftermarket is growing on price with little volume.** Q3 2026: Pool Corp sales +4% with 3 points of price; Hayward +6.5% with 5.4 points of price; Fluidra organic +1.3% with price about +3.4% and volume about -2.2% (derived from its two sales bridges); SiteOne organic daily sales -1% with 4 points of price. List increases announced for the 2027 season: Fluidra North America +3.5% to +5%, Pentair +3% to +4% ("more moderate"), Fluidra Europe about +2%; Hayward has not given a figure and says it will pass its IEEPA tariff refunds to US customers, about 1% of price over six months (10-Q).
- 2026-11-25 — **Channel inventory came out in 2026, unevenly.** Pentair Pool sales -17.7% in Q3 and -18% to -19% for the year after "the 2 largest distributors" removed about US$280m of its inventory over Q2 and Q3; it says the destock is complete and that it will "compete to recover any lost share". Hayward North America +8.7% and Fluidra North America +2.6% in the same quarter, so the cut fell on Pentair. Pool Corp's inventory was down 3% on the year at September with sales up 4%, and its payables down 7%. For 2027 the question is whether sell-in catches up with sell-through (Pentair's plan: about +14% Pool sales on flat sell-through) or distributors cut again; Pool Corp declined to give an inventory target.
- 2026-11-25 — **Structure.** Leslie's filed for Chapter 11 on 2026-09-30 (prearranged; about 76 more stores closed; equity to be cancelled; emergence planned for early 2027). Pool Corp agreed on 2026-11-16 to buy Team Horner (18 HornerXpress centres, 17 in Florida, plus the AquaCal AutoPilot heat-pump and chlorinator business and fiberglass-pool plants in France; price not disclosed; closing Q1 2027) and is "evaluating" the manufacturing parts. Heritage Pool Supply (Home Depot's SRS) made no pool acquisition in October-November; Home Depot's call did not mention Heritage. In Europe, Fluidra says it takes less price because of Chinese entrants; Maytronics reported European sales down 15% in local currency in Q2 (press) and FIMI has offered to take control. Fluidra holds 27% of Aiper, one of the entrants; the step to a majority is no longer expected in 2027.
- 2026-11-25 — **Tariff refunds are in 2026 margins.** Refunds of IEEPA tariffs after the US Supreme Court ruling of 2026-02-20: Pentair about US$43m year to date (US$8m in Q3), Fluidra about EUR 8m gross and EUR 6m net in Q3, Hayward passing its benefit to customers. These do not repeat in 2027.
