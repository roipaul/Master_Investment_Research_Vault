# China consumer cycle

Where Chinese consumer demand stands, with two policy-driven sub-cycles: the trade-in subsidy for appliances and electronics, and the subsidy war in food delivery and instant commerce. Bears on [[JD]], [[3690-HK]], [[0700-HK]], [[TME]], [[TCOM]], [[ZTO]] and [[YMM]].

## Evidence log

- 2026-11-19 — [[JD]] Q3 2026 read. **Durables / trade-in: Trough, from Downturn.** JD's electronics and home appliances revenue was -0.7% YoY after -11.8% in Q2 as the 2025 subsidy base normalises; general merchandise +8.1%; advertising up double digits. The macro is still slowing: NBS retail sales +0.4% YoY in October (September +1.1%), online goods +2.9% for January-October against +3.8% for January-September, communication equipment +6.5% in October from +31.1% in September. The full-year RMB 250bn of trade-in funds is fully allocated (NDRC, October) and nothing official exists on 2027; JD's CEO flagged demand "brought forward by the subsidy". **Food-delivery and instant-commerce subsidy war: Rolling over from the Q3 2025 peak.** JD's food-delivery loss is down more than 50% YoY and 7% QoQ, group marketing expense -26%, and the CFO committed to cut food-delivery investment "significantly" in 2027. June-quarter peers: Alibaba quick-commerce losses "substantially narrowed" (profit targeted FY2029), Meituan core local commerce back to a 7.9% margin but subsidies "still much higher than 2024" with a few quarters to normalise; PDD stays out. Alibaba and PDD report 2026-11-19 and Meituan on 2026-11-27: re-read then. Full note: [[earnings/FY2026-Q3]] under JD.
- 2026-11-19 — Cross-reference: [[0700-HK]] Q3 2026 said the advertising market was weak "for several months" and payment value per transaction still falling, which fits the NBS slowdown. JD's advertising (+9.6%) and Tencent's (+19%) both outgrew Alibaba's customer management revenue (+1% like for like, June quarter).
- 2026-11-20 — [[PDD]] Q3 2026 read. Online marketing +12% (from +3%) while [[JD]] advertising grew double digits and Alibaba's customer management revenue was +1% like for like (-5% reported; management: consumption "remained under pressure" into December, consumers "more cautious", staples ahead of discretionary). Platform advertising is diverging: the platforms that cut merchant fees in 2024-25 (PDD) or can show returns (JD) are taking ad budgets, not the market growing. PDD sales and marketing -17% and transaction services -12% in the first quarter of the EU €3 duty. **Subsidy contest: Rolling over** (Alibaba plans to halve quick-commerce losses next year; PDD stays out). Full note: [[earnings/FY2026-Q3]] under PDD.
