# China express delivery cycle

Volume growth, price per parcel and profit per parcel in Chinese express delivery, and the "anti-involution" campaign against below-cost pricing that began in mid-2025. Bears on [[ZTO]] and on JD Logistics inside [[JD]]; freight read-across to [[YMM]].

## Evidence log

- 2026-11-19 — Industry and peers, read for [[ZTO]] Q3 2026 (ZTO's own update is paused until its Q3 deck can be read; see `monitoring.md`). **Volume growth is slowing and the year-on-year price gain is narrowing.** State Post Bureau: parcel volume +5.0% in H1 2026, about +3.0% in Q3 (computed from published totals; ZTO and YTO both imply +2.9%), +2.1% in September, and "slightly up" in October with revenue up about 3% (SPB index report, 17 Nov; formal October release not yet out). Revenue per parcel about +3.7% YoY in Q3 (computed), and the gap between revenue and volume growth narrowed from 4.2 points in August to 3.5 in September as the August-September 2025 price rises are lapped. SPB says revenue per parcel "keeps returning to a reasonable range". Regional price rises were reported in Henan, Jiangsu and Anhui in September; no official price floor or penalty for 2026 was found. **Share:** against industry growth of about 3%, September volumes were STO +15.4% (helped by the Daniao acquisition), YTO +12.3%, J&T China +15.3% (Q3), ZTO +7.0% (Q3, share 20.1%), Yunda +1.4%. **Price per parcel, September YoY:** YTO -5.8%, Yunda +5.9%, STO +2.4%. **Profit:** Q3 net profit YTO +39%, Yunda +28%, STO +79%, each slower than its nine-month rate. Sources: SPB releases of 17 Jul, 18 Sep, 19 Oct and 17 Nov 2026 (spb.gov.cn, Chinese); company monthly operating data and Q3 reports via Sohu, Jiemian, The Paper (Chinese); J&T press release of 15 Oct 2026.
- 2026-11-19 — [[ZTO]] Q3 2026 release and call, facts only (no thesis call made): parcel volume 10.24bn (+7.0%), revenue RMB 14.4bn (+21.0%, mostly key-account and reverse-logistics revenue booked gross), adjusted net income RMB 2.57bn (+2.6%; +20.7% excluding last year's RMB 376m tax refund), full-year volume guidance of +6% to +10% kept (it was +10% to +13% in May and was cut in August). Management on the call: slower volume growth will "become the industry norm"; prices softened in the low season and are recovering in Yiwu and Hebei into peak; social-insurance compliance for couriers will pressure per-parcel cost in the near term; no dividend for 2026 because buybacks (funded by the February convertible) exceed 100% of last year's profit. Sources: [6-K Exhibit 99.1, 2026-11-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001677250/000110465926129305/tm2630022d1_ex99-1.htm); [call transcript, Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/18/zto-express-zto-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/).
- 2026-11-19 — [[JD]] Q3 2026 read: JD Logistics revenue +16.1% YoY with operating income +49.8% and a 3.0% margin "despite higher oil prices"; external customers about 69% of revenue (computed from press reports of its HKEX announcement). ZTO put the diesel effect at about RMB 0.01 per parcel in Q3 and expects the same or slightly more in Q4.
