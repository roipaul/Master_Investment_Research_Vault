**Provisional baseline (Claude's framing, not Paul's view).** The return depends on: (1) core local commerce (CLC) operating margin rebuilding from single digits toward a lower plateau than the 20.9% of 2024, put at 13-16% in a regulated duopoly, because merchants and users now use more than one app and rider insurance is permanent; (2) food-delivery order share holding at or above about 45% with a higher GTV share; (3) in-store, hotel and travel holding its margin against Douyin and Amap, since it carries most of CLC's profit; (4) New Initiatives losses capped at the 2025 level (RMB 10.1bn) and then falling; (5) cash and the RMB 77bn stake portfolio going back to shareholders once the war is over.

What to track: CLC margin; food-delivery unit economics and order share; sales and marketing as a share of revenue; in-store margin commentary and Douyin's scale; New Initiatives loss; Alibaba's and JD's food-delivery spending; buybacks and debt.

What would break it (from the September catch-up): order share below about 45%; food-delivery unit economics not improving year on year through H2 2026; Alibaba re-escalating in instant retail.

Missing: Paul's view, debate with probabilities, variant view, model.

## 1. Bottom line

**Thesis: Intact (against the provisional baseline), with the margin leg on guidance but far from the plateau.** CLC earned RMB 5.7bn (7.3% margin) in the seasonal peak quarter, a RMB 19.8bn swing from a year ago and about RMB 1.6bn better than Visible Alpha consensus (press). Revenue grew 21.6%. But the margin slipped from 7.9% in Q2, sales and marketing was still 23.4% of revenue, and outside estimates put food-delivery profit at about RMB 0.1-0.2 an order against roughly RMB 1 before the war. (Claude's read)

**Fresh money at about HK$65.6 (intraday, 2026-11-27): Wait.** The base case returns about 16% a year to 2029, just above the 15% hurdle, but the conservative case returns about 3% and the harsh case loses about half. The price already assumes CLC margins rebuild to about 10-12% (section 6). What would settle it: the call transcript (in-store margins, Q4 and 2027 unit economics) and Q4 results in about late March 2027, where CLC needs to hold a margin of about 6% or better in the seasonally weaker quarter with sales and marketing below about 23% of revenue. On price alone, the conservative case clears 15% at about HK$48. (Claude's read)

## 3. Structural changes: puts and takes

**A. Food delivery is profitable again, but at a fraction of its old unit profit.**
- What changed: CLC moved from a RMB 14.1bn loss to a RMB 5.7bn profit year on year. Outside estimates of food-delivery profit per order in Q3: Citi about RMB 0.08 (operating margin about 1.1%), Guonong Securities about RMB 0.2 (press). Goldman's November estimate of Q3 daily orders: Meituan 84m (about 48%), Taobao Instant Commerce 75m (42%), JD 18m (10%).
- Puts: the war is over in the price of an order (average order value up, cheap orders down); regulation caps the return of mass subsidies; Meituan kept about half of orders and more of GTV.
- Takes: unit profit is perhaps a tenth to a fifth of the pre-war level; rider insurance and heat subsidies are permanent; Alibaba holds four in ten orders with its own reasons to stay.
- Net effect (e): at 84m orders a day, each RMB 0.5 of profit per order is about RMB 15bn a year of operating profit (84m x 365 x 0.5). Moving from about RMB 0.15 to about RMB 0.65 per order by 2029 is most of what the base case needs.
- Updates: leg 1 and leg 2.
- Confidence: medium. The per-order figures are broker estimates; the call should give a direction.

**B. Growth is shifting to goods Meituan owns.**
- What changed: self-operated goods in CLC +75.8%, goods in New Initiatives +48.6% (press); Xiaoxiang 85 cities with 9 offline stores; Dingdong pending.
- Puts: more orders through the same rider network; grocery profitable in mature cities.
- Takes: reported revenue growth overstates platform growth; capital and inventory needs rise; the Meituan Select lesson.
- Net effect: not sized until revenue by type is read.
- Updates: primer question 5 (grocery returns).

**C. New Initiatives losses are contained while Keeta expands.**
- What changed: segment loss flat year on year at RMB 1.3bn on +32.9% revenue; Keeta profitable in Hong Kong and in Saudi Arabia for the full quarter; Brazil at 32 cities (company press release).
- Puts: the 2026 cap of RMB 10.1bn will be beaten by about RMB 3-4bn (e, if Q4 loss is about RMB 2bn).
- Takes: Brazil spending rises from here; iFood holds about 80% (press).
- Updates: leg 4.

**D. Unresolved: in-store under a second attack.** No figure in the release; Douyin's deals app at more than 20m DAU (press). Carried as structural-or-cyclical until the call and Q4 show the in-store margin.

**Expected by now and not seen:** any return of capital beyond the June buyback; instead, new debt.

## 4. The cycle

Food delivery and in-store are not driven by an economic cycle so much as by a cycle of competitive spending. The indicators below are set on this first run and carried each quarter.

| Indicator | Q3 2026 | Q2 2026 | Q3 2025 | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sales and marketing % revenue | 23.4% | 23.6% | 35.9% | Flat QoQ, down YoY |
| CLC operating margin | 7.3% | 7.9% | (20.9%) | Down QoQ (seasonal, guided), up YoY |
| Delivery services revenue growth (incentives are netted here) | +25.3% | +17.5% | (17.1%) | Up |
| Food-delivery profit per order (broker estimates) | ~RMB 0.08-0.2 | ~RMB 0.16 | loss of ~RMB 3-4 | Flat |
| Meituan order share (Goldman) | ~48% | ~49% (June) | n/r | Flat |
| JD New Businesses loss (RMB bn) | 9.6 | 9.9 | 15.7 | Down |
| NBS retail sales growth | +0.4% (Oct) | n/r | n/r | Slowing (from [[china-consumer-cycle]]) |

**Peers (same set each quarter):** [[JD]] Q3 2026 (food-delivery loss down more than 50% YoY, a "significant" cut planned for 2027); Alibaba September quarter 2026 (quick-commerce revenue RMB 58.8bn, +22%, unit economics improving, and per [[china-consumer-cycle]] a plan to halve quick-commerce losses next year); Dingdong Q3 2026 (11th profitable quarter, RMB 298m net income; read for grocery). All three point the same way: spending is falling at every player, none is re-escalating.

**Phase:**
- Food-delivery subsidy contest: **Early recovery** (last: Rolling over, per the 2026-11-19 cycle note). Profit is back, unit economics are thin, and the next step depends on whether the last 4 points of marketing come out.
- In-store, hotel and travel: **Unresolved**, leaning to rising competitive spend (Douyin's app; management's guided H2 margin decline).
- Consumer backdrop: slowing (NBS October retail +0.4%).

**Earnings against mid-cycle:** CLC at 7.3% against the provisional plateau of 13-16% is under-earning by about half; group earnings (about RMB 7bn adjusted annualised, e) are well below the base case's 2029 RMB 33bn.

**What the price assumes:** about a 10-12% CLC margin by 2029 (section 6): a recovery, but not back to the plateau.

**Next period:** confirm the phase with Q4 sales and marketing below about 23% of revenue and a CLC margin of about 6% or better; the phase is wrong if marketing rises again in Q4 (outside the summer peak) or Alibaba's December-quarter quick-commerce losses widen.

**What the price implies.** Working backwards with the base case's other lines and a 15x exit: for a 10% annual return the price needs a 2029 CLC margin of about 12%; crediting the stake portfolio at management's value, about 10%. For the 15% hurdle it needs about 13.7%. So the market is already paying for a large part of the recovery from 7.3%, and the stock only clears the hurdle if the margin reaches the middle of the provisional plateau.

**Entry levels.** The base case clears 15% at about HK$67 (at today's price, roughly). The conservative case clears 15% at about HK$48 (72.7 / 1.15^3).

**Consensus.** One feed (MarketScreener, read 2026-11-24) shows EPS of RMB 3.42 for 2027 and RMB 5.64 for 2028, i.e. about RMB 34bn of 2028 net profit, above this framing's 2029 base. At HK$65.6 the stock is about 10.7x 2028 consensus. Post-print revisions not yet available.

**Reaction to the print.** Not yet known: the results came after the Hong Kong close on a Friday. Before the print the shares rose about 4-5% on 2026-11-26 and again on 2026-11-27 intraday (Zhitong), from about HK$73 on 2026-11-11 (etnet).

**Fresh money: Wait.** Meeting the stock today: at about HK$66 you buy a company back to profit in its two main segments, with RMB 157bn of cash and short-term deposits and a stake portfolio management values at RMB 77bn, whose core earnings are at about half of where a regulated duopoly should settle. What has to be true is a CLC margin back to about 14% by 2029; the evidence so far is three quarters of correct guidance, rivals cutting spend, and regulation against subsidies, but also marketing stuck at 23% of revenue and an in-store fight that has not yet shown in the numbers. The base case clears the hurdle only narrowly, the conservative case does not, and the harsh case halves the money. With no transcript, no in-store number and no variant view, the call is Wait on evidence. It moves to Own if the call and Q4 show in-store margins holding and marketing falling, or if the price falls toward about HK$48 where the conservative case clears the hurdle. (Claude's read)
