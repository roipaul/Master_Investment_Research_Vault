## 1. Reads (newest first)

### 2026-11-28 - first read

All of this section is inference from the event log, apart from the dated readings under Sentiment.

**Focus.** Since 5 November the price has responded to two things, and neither is the earnings.
1. *Who runs the company and what the investigations find.* Eight of the last nine rows are governance, legal or export news: the board inquiry (E13), the downgrades that followed (E14), the resignation (E15), the SEC and short-seller reports around results (E16), the state subpoena (E17), the SEC accounting report (E18), the reinstatement (E19) and the days after (E20).
2. *Export enforcement.* The Senate summons in June (E8) and the halt of server shipments to four Southeast Asian countries in the last week (E20).

Before November the variable was a different one: whether customers can keep paying for the build-out. The stock fell on a record quarter in February with commentary on hyperscaler cash flow (E3), and fell with the sector on financing scares (E11) and on the midterm result (E12).

**What it ignores.**
- Beats and raised guides. A US$3bn revenue beat in February: −5.5% (E3). A beat, a US$91bn guide against about US$86bn to US$87bn expected, an US$80bn buyback and a dividend raised from US$0.01 to US$0.25 in May: −1.8% (E6). A 6% revenue beat and a higher FY28 outlook in November: −2.9% (E16).
- Capital return (E6).
- The one exception is E10 in August, +8.7%: the new information was FY28 growth of about 70% against about 44% that investors expected. That was news about how long the growth lasts, not about the quarter.

**Earnings or discount.** The discount.
- Estimates rose while the price fell. Consensus EPS is US$9.67 for FY27 and US$16.61 for FY28 (StockAnalysis, 2026-11-24). The forward multiple went from 19.9x (Motley Fool, 2026-04-03, then called the lowest in two years) to about 24x (XTB, 2026-08-26), about 15x (24/7 Wall St, 2026-11-06) and 10.3x (StockAnalysis, 2026-11-25 close).
- Split since early April, the earliest dated forward multiple read: the price is down about 16% (US$177.39 on 2026-04-02 to US$149.30 on 2026-11-25) and the multiple is down about 48%, so forward earnings are up about 62% on this arithmetic. The whole fall, and more, is the multiple. A forward estimate from a year ago was not available.
- The news that moves the stock is governance, legal, export policy and financing risk, which is the discount pattern.

**The story.** The market's working story appears to be that next year's earnings are not in doubt but what they are worth is. The company turned out to depend on one person; it is under SEC, state and congressional scrutiny; export rules are tightening; and the financing of its customers looked shaky before any of that. The price now follows governance and legal headlines, and results no longer move it. Confidence: medium. The pattern is only four weeks old, and one day (E19) was described by a strategist as "mechanical" buying.

Two readings that would show the story has changed:
1. The stock holds or rises through a bad legal or export headline. That would be the washed-out signature, which the log does not yet show (E20).
2. The stock starts to move on estimate news again, such as the hyperscalers' 2027 capex guides or the Q4 print, while governance news stops moving it. The argument would have gone back to earnings.

**Sentiment position: Washed out, and unstable.** No earlier state is on file. The evidence is mixed and is set out so the call can be checked.

*Reaction asymmetry.*
- From 5 to 20 November the stock fell on every kind of news, including a beat with a raised outlook (E16).
- It then rose 31.5% on one governance headline (E19) and gave back 9.5% in the next two sessions on export news (E20).
- It has not yet stopped falling on bad news, so the cleanest washed-out test is not met.

*Why Washed out all the same.* The drawdown, the seven-week run and a forward multiple at less than half its August level are extremes against this stock's own recent history. Against that, four-fifths of the sell-side still rate it a buy with targets 60% above the price, and short interest is small. A reader who weights the rating mix more would say Sceptical.
