# PDD — what Paul's research folder adds (2026-11-22)

Summaries and judgments only. Sell-side and transcript text stays in the folder; files are cited by name. Figures marked (VA) are Visible Alpha consensus as of 22 Nov 2026; GMV and Temu figures are sell-side estimates, not company data. (e) = Claude's computation or estimate.

## Bottom line

1. **Consensus sits above our base case, and most of the gap is Temu.** Consensus non-GAAP net income is RMB 104.6bn FY26E, 121.2bn FY27E, 139.0bn FY28E, 146.5bn FY29E (VA). Our earnings-update base case has RMB 130.5bn in 2029, so consensus gets there a year earlier. At the same 10x exit, consensus FY29E gives about 20% a year from RMB 576.9 against our 15% (e). Consensus has Temu's operating result swinging from −10.9bn (FY26E) to −0.6bn (FY27E) and +7.6bn (FY28E). That RMB 18.5bn swing is more than half of the RMB 34bn rise in consensus net income FY26E–FY28E (e, before tax on Temu profit). Domestic online marketing grows about 7% a year (232.1bn → 265.4bn). So the Street's case is "domestic steady, Temu turns"; ours is "domestic recovers, Temu narrows".
2. **Temu breakeven has slid about a year every year.** Morgan Stanley (Oct 2023) expected Temu profitable from 2026. UBS (Sep 2025) expected quarterly breakeven by end-2026, with a 6% margin by 2029 against 4% for consensus then. Consensus now has a small loss in FY27E. Over about 15 months, consensus FY27 revenue fell 16% and FY27 non-GAAP net income fell 19%. (Claude's read: treat any Temu breakeven date as soft.)
3. **The Q3 2026 domestic recovery is a take-rate story, not a volume story.** Online marketing ÷ sell-side domestic GMV (e): 4.16% FY23, 4.55% FY24, 4.46% FY25, 4.31% Q3'25, 4.50% Q3'26. Estimated domestic GMV growth slowed from +17.7% (FY24) to +12.2% (FY25) and +7.5% (Q3'26). Since April 2026, the platform pricing rules require consultation before fee changes. That caps the lever that drove Q3. This sharpens debate question 1.
4. **Management has not mentioned capital return once in seven calls (Q1'25–Q3'26).** There are zero hits for buyback, repurchase or dividend. On the Q4'25 call (25 Mar 2026, new to us), management says PDD is "still in a strategic investment phase", competition "has continued to intensify", and the aim is to "build another Pinduoduo" within three years by investing in the supply chain rather than diversifying. Restricted net assets rose from RMB 10.8bn (2020) to RMB 125.9bn (2025). This sharpens debate question 5.
5. **Paul's 2022 skepticism was wrong on the mechanism, and the same frame is being applied again.** The May 2022 valuation note, leaning on Morningstar, expected EBITDA ÷ GMV of 0.7% by 2030, a 14% operating margin by 2031, about 10% GMV growth and share falling to 15%. The outturn: about 1.8% operating profit ÷ GMV in FY25 (e), a non-GAAP operating margin of about 30% in FY24, and sell-side domestic GMV up about 25% a year 2020–25 (e). The miss was assuming growth was bought with subsidies and would stall when they stopped. Instead, a low-take-rate ad model monetised the traffic. The 2025–26 bear case ("the RMB 100bn programme buys growth that won't last") has the same shape. That is worth testing, not assuming. (Claude's read)

  - Tencent: 13.8%.

### Long-form (`Notes and Transcripts/`)
- **Business Breakdowns (Colossus, about 2021, with PDD corporate development).**
  - Revenue is about 90% ads on top of a 0.6% transaction fee that covers payments.
  - Moat in management's words: "easy to copy the form but not the spirit"; mobile-only from day one.
  - First-party sales were called "a stop-gap … not a very permanent feature". The about RMB 100bn first-party brand push since 2025 reverses that. (Claude's read: a real strategy shift, worth asking about in the debate.)
  - Duoduo Maicai pickup economics: the last mile is about a third of logistics cost.
- **Acquired (2020).** History, already in the primer. Tencent made PDD the kingmaker's pick in Feb 2017 through WeChat access. Founding take rate was 0.6%, against Taobao about 5% and JD about 28% (revenue ÷ GMV including first-party).
- **TIP podcast (2026, value-investor pitch).**
  - Cheap: under 8x earnings, cash about 60% of market value. Li Lu and Norbert Lu are holders.
  - Fair value about US$100 base and US$50 bear, with cash haircut 17% in the base case.
  - The co-host passed: retail with falling growth, almost no disclosure, and "unlikely investors will see the cash".
  - It names PDD's pattern of serial reinvestment: core marketplace → Duoduo Maicai → Temu → now supply chain and brands. That is the strongest version of the bear case on our question 5.

Claude's read:
- The sell-side has been mostly Buy for eight years. Meanwhile the stock has gone sideways since 2023 and estimates have fallen.
