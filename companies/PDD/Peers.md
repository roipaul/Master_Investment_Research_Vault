## Findings

1. **"Peers that return cash" is a weaker set than it looks in 2026.** Tencent halved its buyback to fund AI; Alibaba became a net issuer (US$10bn placement); Baidu's buyback is under 1%. The real returners are NetEase (2.2% dividend + buybacks, 38% net cash), JD (3.9% dividend, but thin margins and a European acquisition) and Vipshop (high payout, small and ex-growth). (fact + inference)
2. **PDD is the cheapest on earnings ex cash by a wide margin**: ~3.4x vs NetEase ~7x, Tencent ~8x ex portfolio, Alibaba ~11x headline. (e)
3. **Both groups carry a capital-allocation discount, of different kinds.** For Tencent, Alibaba and Baidu the cash goes into AI capex the market can see but cannot yet value; for PDD the cash sits idle and the risk is management intent (New Pinmu, no payout). PDD's cash is intact; theirs is being spent. (inference)
4. **Closest clean comparator is NetEase**: net-cash-heavy, already paying, cheap ex cash (~7x), but earnings falling (Q3 revenue −1.8%). It is the benchmark for "what PDD might trade at once it pays": NetEase at ~11x headline vs PDD 8.3x. (e)
5. **Regulatory read-across**: Trip.com's RMB 5.2bn antitrust penalty in 2026 shows enforcement hits peers too, not only PDD.
