## Verdict (proposed)
Business **Inconclusive**; price check **Stretch**. A first-class operating machine priced as if it will never grow and its cash will never count. The return to the 15% hurdle depends less on the business than on whether management lets profit show.

## Thesis in one paragraph
PDD runs China's lowest-cost marketplace, an advertising toll on unbranded factory and farm supply with a marketplace margin near 47% on almost no capital, plus [[Duoduo Maicai]] (now profitable) and Temu (about −RMB 16bn in 2026E). At US$83.68 the market pays about 8x 2026E non-GAAP EPS and about 3.5x ex-cash: it prices flat earnings and cash that never returns. The business is not flat (domestic profit mid-single-digit growth, Maicai compounding, Temu loss per order narrowing), but management has committed two RMB 100bn programmes (merchant support; New Pinmu own brands) and says it remains in a "strategic investment phase". The gap closes only if those programmes stop absorbing the gains.

## Return parts
1. Earnings: EPS RMB 70 (2026E) → 86–98 (2029E) if Temu reaches breakeven, 7–12% a year; conservative ~71.
2. Re-rating 8x → 10x adds ~8 points a year to 2029; needs Temu breakeven and spending visibly past peak.
3. Cash return: none assumed.
Not relied on: buybacks, New Pinmu returns, Temu above breakeven, consensus 2028 RMB 139bn, management's quality claims.

## Falsifiers
1. Online marketing revenue < +3% for two quarters in 2027 with a falling take rate.
2. FY2027 non-GAAP op margin < 25%, or a programme beyond the two RMB 100bn commitments.
3. New Pinmu inventory + prepayments > RMB 30bn by end-2027, or domestic own-brand selling against merchants.
4. HNTE not renewed, or a second case naming PDD for obstruction or fee limits.
5. Transaction services keep falling in 2027 with no rise in group margin.
