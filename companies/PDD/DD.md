# PDD — Desk due diligence (2026-11-23)

Scope run today: desk steps 1 (New Pinmu), 2 (15% high-tech tax status), 3 (Huang's trust) and 8 (Fufeitong), from the plan in chat on 23 Nov. Facts carry sources; inferences are marked. Copyrighted articles are summarised, not reproduced.

## 1. What changed

1. **The 15% tax test moves from April 2027 to late 2027.** (fact + inference)
   - The 20-Fs show three-year certificate cycles: FY2022 20-F said Shanghai Xunmeng's 15% ran "until 2023"; FY2024 and FY2025 say "until 2026" (Xunmeng 2018–2026, Walnut Shanghai 2021–2026). So both were renewed in 2024 and the next renewal is for 2027–2029.
   - Shanghai publishes recognition batches around Oct–Dec. The FY2026 20-F (April 2027) will most likely still say "until 2026"; the real answer comes from the 2027 Shanghai HNTE lists and the FY2027 20-F. (inference)
   - Shanghai Xunmeng is in the first tier of the "2025 Shanghai high-tech enterprise innovation top 100" (Shanghai science-management bodies; via maigoo.com, 5 Nov 2025). A standing signal, not a renewal.
   - **Effect:** Q8 (tax) probability unchanged; the dated catalyst is corrected. Worth about RMB 9.6bn a year if lost.

2. **New Pinmu is two things, and the near-term one is an export-services layer for Temu sellers.** (fact; reading = inference)
   - Two Shanghai companies registered in February 2026, both with Zhao Jiazhen as legal representative: 上海新拼姆鸿乔电子商务 (registered capital RMB 10bn) and 上海新拼姆朴动电子商务 (RMB 5bn) (Ebrun via Sina, 31 Mar 2026; 新莓/36Kr, 19 May 2026; Tech星球/36Kr, 28 May 2026).
   - From mid-April, Temu sellers (apparel, home, general goods) were asked to sign a "foreign-trade comprehensive service agreement" with 朴动: export agency, customs, logistics, payment collection. Proceeds go to New Pinmu first and are settled on actual sales, slower than the fully managed model; clause 11.4 lets it deduct or freeze seller funds (新莓/36Kr).
   - Separately, LatePost (12 May 2026) reports an independent team of about 100, unknown to most PDD/Temu staff, negotiating outright-purchase deals with large factories in apparel, home and outdoor; framed as "a necessary investment for Temu, not yet another startup". The Paper reports part-prepayment at order, first products due in the US and EU in Q3 2026, and no domestic sales for now.
   - Management said on the Q2 2026 call the rollout was "slower than expected".
   - **Effect on Q6 (inference):** near term New Pinmu looks like a Temu supply-chain and settlement layer (seller money flows through PDD, adding float) plus a bounded buyout pilot for export, not a domestic retailer competing with Pinduoduo merchants. Inventory and prepayment risk exists but is small today. Q6 leans "Temu supply-chain play" ~55% (was open). The RMB 100bn three-year figure remains the risk to Q2.

3. **Fufeitong: licence secured long-term; the insider structure is now more entrenched.** (fact; reading = inference)
   - PBOC renewed Fufeitong's payment licence as "long-term valid" in the May 2026 batch (PBOC renewal notice, 30 Apr 2026, via 10jqka/Eastmoney).
   - Chen Lei is actual controller, legal representative and chairman; 上海易翼信息科技 holds 93.89% (21财经 via CNFin, 9 Nov 2026). The 20-F names Ningbo Hexin (93.84%), funded by RMB 910.6m of interest-free PDD loans; the two are presumably the same chain (not checked in the registry).
   - Registered capital raised from RMB 121.65m to RMB 300m in Oct 2025, with Yiyi subscribing pro rata (CNFin).
   - 2025 revenue RMB 1.059bn, net profit RMB 0.33m. China Post Capital is selling 0.6954% at a floor of RMB 6.79m, implying about RMB 976m for the whole company (Shanghai United Assets and Equity Exchange listing, via CNFin).
   - PBOC Shanghai approved senior-management changes in Jan and Sep 2026 (Beijing Business Today, 13 Sep 2026).
   - Fufeitong itself holds a high-tech certificate (GR202331005575; Shanghai rename list, Dec 2025).
   - **Effect (inference):** the licence-renewal trigger that might have forced a buy-in is gone; PDD's right to buy in "subject to approval" is still unexercised. Small in money, telling on governance.

4. **Huang's trust: no visible change; the offshore-trust tax looks a weak threat.** (fact + inference)
   - The last 13G/A found (Amendment No. 2, March 2021) shows the trust with sole voting power over 1,409,744,080 shares, the same count as the FY2025 20-F. No later amendment surfaced in searches; EDGAR itself could not be opened from here.
   - The rule (MOF/STA Announcement 2026 No. 21 and STA No. 15, 24 Jul 2026) taxes at 20% assets placed in an offshore trust in 2023–2025 and trust income before 2026; filing window closed 22 Oct 2026 (Hankun Law, 29 Jul 2026; Southern Metropolis Daily via Eastmoney, 21 Oct 2026).
   - Huang's trust predates 2023 (inference from the 2021 filing) and PDD pays no dividends, so his exposure is probably limited to other trust income we cannot see. The Xueqiu claim that he borrowed to pay tax is **unverified**; no press report found.
   - **Effect:** no change to governance view. Keep watching 13G amendments and pledge footnotes.
