## Thesis in one paragraph

No thesis written yet. Provisional baseline (Claude's Sept 2026 pitch draft for Paul, in the Project chat, not signed off): Tencent is two businesses — a core (games, ads, fintech) compounding earnings at low-to-mid teens via ~10% revenue growth, ~1pt/yr of mix-driven margin expansion and buybacks, and an AI-native business funded as a finite 2026-27 capex "lump sum" whose losses are disclosed separately. The market prices the AI cost without the core's quality or the compute's fallback value (rentable). Pay for the core; treat AI as a free option. Full baseline in [[earnings/FY2026-Q3]].
