**Provisional baseline.** Claude's pitch draft of 2026-09-07 for Paul (this Project's chat), which he challenged but did not sign off:

The return depends on (1) revenue growing ~10% a year (ads 15-20%, domestic games high-single to low-double digits, FBS ~9%); (2) ex-AI non-IFRS operating margin expanding ~1pt a year from mix shift (in-house games, Video Account ads, wealth management growing faster than payments and long-form video); (3) the AI spend being a finite 2026-27 "lump sum", with the new-AI-products loss stabilising around RMB 10-12bn a quarter; (4) buybacks of HK$80-100bn a year.

What to track: ex-AI operating profit and margin; the AI-products drag; capex and FCF ex-prepayments; Video Accounts time spent vs impressions; cloud growth and AI revenue disclosure; domestic games receipts vs revenue; buyback pace.

What would break it: ex-AI margin flat for two quarters; AI drag past ~RMB 15bn a quarter with no revenue to show; 2027 capex at or above 2026; buybacks near zero; Video Accounts time spent slowing while load rises; ad growth below 10% without a visible macro shock.

Missing: triage, debate with probabilities, variant view, model, verified portfolio value.

## 1. Bottom line

**Thesis: Weakened.** The core engine did not break but it stalled this quarter: ex-AI operating margin was flat YoY at 40% after +2.8pts in Q2, the AI drag jumped to ~RMB 15bn (from 10.5bn), cash capex is "at peak" and stays there through 2027, and the Q3 buyback was cut by more than half. Ads still grew 19% and domestic games 11%. (Claude's read)

**Fresh money at HK$350 (2026-11-19): Wait.** The base case returns about 19% a year over three years at this price, so price is not the problem. The thesis has weakened on three of its six falsifiers at once, and the question that decides it — whether the core margin resumes expanding once DRAM costs and mini-drama start-up costs pass, while the AI loss stops widening — can be read at FY2026 results in mid-March 2027. Two settling readings: Q4 ex-AI operating margin back above 41% with the AI drag at or below RMB 15bn; and a full-year buyback of at least HK$50bn or a stated 2027 figure. On price alone, the conservative case clears 15% a year at about HK$285 or below. (Claude's read)

## 3. Structural changes: puts and takes

**A. AI capex plateau at ~RMB 65bn cash per quarter through 2027 (release + Bernstein Q&A, 2026-11-18)**
- Puts: cloud growth 35% and accelerating; WorkBuddy, MaaS and GPU rental all growing multiples QoQ; management says the compute is fungible and rentable; prepayments (the FCF hit) fall from here.
- Takes: ~RMB 500bn (e) of capex over 2026-27 against an investment portfolio of ~RMB 900bn carrying value; net debt for the first time in a decade; D&A rising ~RMB 50bn/yr (e) by 2028 — ~5pts of group operating margin if none is offset by AI revenue; buybacks funded from the balance sheet and divestments rather than FCF.
- Net effect: reported non-IFRS operating margin stays in the low-to-mid 30s through 2027 (e) even if the core recovers; ex-AI margin is the only line that can show the core. Confidence that the spend ends in 2027: medium — management has been consistent, but has also widened the envelope every quarter.
- Updates: open question "is the capex a lump sum or a run-rate" — now "two-year plateau, decline from 2028". Confirm: Q4 cash capex ≤ 65bn and a 2027 figure. Reverse: 2027 capex guided above 2026 or a new "model training" tranche.

**B. New-AI-products drag ~RMB 15bn/quarter and rising (computed, call)**
- Puts: first quarter with AI revenue visible in the numbers (cloud +35%; WorkBuddy >5x QoQ); the exclusion is now formally defined; spend is being reallocated (Yuanbao cut, WorkBuddy up).
- Takes: the drag has grown 73% in two quarters; no timeline; the 2026 run-rate is ~RMB 50bn pre-tax (e), 17% of core operating profit.
- Net effect: reported EPS stays below ex-AI EPS by ~HK$5/share (e) in 2027. Confidence that it stabilises near 15bn: low-medium — Xiaowei's rollout to 1.4bn users is still ahead, though management says its cost stays below Yuanbao's early-2026 level.
- Updates: falsifier 2. Confirm stabilisation: Q4 drag ≤ 15bn. Reverse: > 18bn.

**C. Mini-drama content costs enter the ad line (CFO, UBS Q&A)**
- Puts: mini dramas "contributed substantially" to ad revenue growth; AI tools power ~80% of mini-drama publishers' monetisation; a new ad-demand category when e-commerce and consumer advertisers are weak.
- Takes: ad gross margin 52% vs 57% a quarter ago — Tencent now pays for content in a segment that had none; management calls the costs front-loaded, which is a claim.
- Net effect: if ad GM settles at 54% (e) rather than 57%, ~RMB 6-7bn/yr less gross profit on 2027E ad revenue of ~RMB 200bn (e). Confidence: medium that part of the drop is permanent.
- Updates: the ad-quality leg of the thesis. Confirm: ad GM back above 55% by Q2 2027. Reverse: below 52%.

**D. Buyback halved (release)**
- Puts: capital redirected to capex with a stated return test; dividend unchanged.
- Takes: ~1.5pts/yr less EPS growth at the Q3 pace; the stock at a 2.5-year low is when a buyback adds most value per remaining share.
- Net effect: EPS compounding 1-1.5pts lower than the baseline until pace resumes. Updates falsifier 4. Confirm: FY2026 total ≥ HK$50bn. Reverse: Q4 near zero.

**E. Positive structural changes the thesis expected and that showed up**
- Video Accounts: impressions +50% YoY with time spent +20% — load is rising fast and engagement is holding. Time per DAU "less than half" of industry leaders — the runway claim is intact.
- In-house game mix: VAS GM +3pts to 64% on internally developed games; Delta Force DAU +50% YoY; domestic games +11%.

**Expected but did not show up:** operating leverage in the core (ex-AI profit grew in line with revenue); FBS gross margin improvement (flat at 50%, down 2pts QoQ); any disclosure of Video Accounts' share of ad revenue.

**Peers.** TME (Q3 2026, [[TME]]): organic membership −0.6% YoY (e), ad revenue falling — consistent with Tencent's weak ad market and consumption read, and with ByteDance taking time. [[NVDA]] FY27 Q3 (theme [[ai-infrastructure-cycle]]): hyperscaler capex +108% YoY, GPU rental rates rising, memory taking margin (SK Hynix 80% operating margin) — consistent with Tencent's DRAM-cost complaint and its claim that compute pricing is "elevated" in 2026-27. Not yet read: NetEase (2026-11-19), Alibaba cloud, Kuaishou ads, Meituan — add after 2026-11-28.

**Phase.**
- China consumer / ad market: last period **Downturn** (choppy); now **Downturn, possibly near trough** — management says ad market weak "for several months" but sees 2027 "more upside than downside" with no base-effect pressure. Evidence for trough: that statement; against: payments value/txn still falling, ads down QoQ. Pace: worse than the Aug update implied.
- AI compute cost cycle: **Peak** — DRAM flat after +400%, management expects compute pricing to fall "significantly" by 2028, cash capex at peak.

**Earnings against mid-cycle.** Under-earning. Core (ex-AI) margin 40% vs 42-43% in H1 and a mid-cycle of ~42% (e): ~RMB 4-5bn/quarter of cyclical shortfall from DRAM and ad weakness. Reported earnings are further depressed by the AI drag, which is a policy, not a cycle.

**What the price assumes.** At HK$350 the market pays ~7-8x 2026E ex-AI core earnings after the portfolio (section 6), which prices the core as ex-growth and the AI spend as value-destroying. It assumes neither the margin nor the buyback comes back.

**Next period (Q4 2026, March 2027).** Confirms the read: ex-AI margin ≥ 41%, ad GM ≥ 54%, marketing revenue QoQ up in the seasonally strong Q4, payments value/txn decline narrowing again. Phase wrong if: ad growth < 15%, ex-AI margin < 40%, or management extends "next few quarters" of pressure.

**Share-price move since the print:** −2.4% in the US on 18 Nov, −6.1% in Hong Kong on 19 Nov to a 2.5-year low. The market took the revenue miss, the AI loss jump and the net-debt position as confirmation that the AI spend is open-ended; it gave no credit to cloud +35% or ex-AI profit +8%.

**Fresh-money view.** Someone meeting Tencent today gets a business at ~7x core earnings after the portfolio, with a 1.45bn-user platform whose ad inventory is still a fraction monetised (impressions +50%, load far below peers), the leading domestic games franchise growing 11%, and a cloud business accelerating to 35%. What has to be true: that the core margin resumes expanding once DRAM and mini-drama costs normalise; that the AI loss stops widening near RMB 15bn/quarter and the capex does come down in 2028; and that capital returns resume. The evidence on file: the ad-load runway is confirmed and strengthening; AI revenue has started to show; but this quarter the margin stalled, the loss widened 45%, the buyback halved and the balance sheet went to net debt — all in the same print, and management gave no timeline on the loss ("no one knows"). The price is attractive and the harsh case (−16% over three years) is bearable. What is missing is one more reading on the core margin and the AI envelope.

**Call: Wait.** Thesis Weakened rules out Own under the skill's conditions, and between Own and Wait the cautious call stands. What moves it to Own: at FY2026 results (mid-March 2027), Q4 ex-AI operating margin ≥ 41% with the AI drag ≤ RMB 15bn and a Q4 buyback that makes the FY total ≥ HK$50bn — or, on price alone, HK$285 or below. What moves it to Don't own: ex-AI margin below 40% again (falsifier 1 triggered), AI drag above RMB 18bn, or 2027 capex guided above 2026.

Comparison with the earlier (Sept, provisional) verdict: the Sept draft said "constructive, accumulate" at HK$489 on the two-business framing. The framing held — the core is cheaper and ads stronger than then — but two of its load-bearing assumptions (margin expansion continuing, drag stabilising) did not. The Sept call was early rather than wrong on price; it was wrong on the drag. Wait is the right call now because the stock has fallen 28% and the thesis has weakened: the price is better and the evidence is worse, and one more quarter resolves which matters more.

**Model.** No model on file. **Build due** before any decision: a value DCF with the core and the AI business separated, the portfolio as a separate line, and the capex/D&A schedule explicit (2026-27 plateau, 2028 decline).
