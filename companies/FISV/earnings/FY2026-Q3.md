**Provisional baseline.** This is Claude's framing. Paul owns FISV but has not written down a view.

The return depends on three things:
1. The 2026 trough holds and revenue grows again: organic growth to mid-single digits from Q4 2026, toward management's 4-6% CAGR for 2026-29.
2. Margins recover from about 31% toward the 37% 2029 target, through Project Elevate savings that reach profit rather than being reinvested or competed away.
3. Cash flow of about 90% of adjusted net income goes to buybacks at a low multiple, with leverage held near 3x.

What to track:
- Organic growth by segment
- Clover revenue against Clover volume (GPV)
- Banking attrition and Financial Solutions growth
- Adjusted operating margin, and how much it relies on add-backs
- Free cash flow conversion
- Leverage and buybacks

What would break it:
- Another cut to guidance, or a 2027 guide below 2% organic growth.
- Merchant organic growth negative again in Q4 2026.
- 2027 adjusted margin no higher than 2026.
- Leverage above 3.5x reported, or a rating downgrade while buybacks continue.

Missing: primer, triage, debate with probabilities, variant view, model.

## 1. Bottom line

But the Merchant leg is weaker than the plan needs, and the peer read says its problem is Fiserv's own, not the market's. (Claude's read)

**Fresh money at US$62.79 (2026-11-17): Wait.** The base case returns about 21% a year and meets the hurdle. ## 3. Structural changes: puts and takes

**A. Clover is growing volume but not revenue.**
- **What changed:**
  - Clover revenue +1% (Q2 +2%; Q4 2025 +12%; 2025 +23%) against GPV +11% ex-gateway.
  - Revenue per dollar of volume is falling.
  - VAS revenue −3%, stable excluding Argentina (claim).
  - Hardware (product) revenue US$2,922m YTD vs US$3,287m, −11%.
  - Non-Clover SMB −6%.
- **Puts:**
  - Volume still grows roughly in line with the US card market: Visa US volume +10%.
  - Clover processing revenue +12%.
  - Over 5m devices shipped.
  - Clover for Enterprise opens a new market in 2027.
  - Q4 laps the 2025 fee removals (2-point drag in Q4 2025) and Argentina's peak.
- **Takes:**
  - Toast grew GPV 20% and raised its take rate, citing share gains among larger restaurants.
  - GPN's Genius bookings more than doubled, and GPN is rolling Genius out to more than 30 bank partners, which is Clover's distribution model.
  - Visa says the US consumer is steady, so a weak cycle does not explain −2% in Merchant.
  - The 2025 hardware and fee push looks like borrowed revenue now being repaid.
- **Net effect:**
  - Base case: Clover revenue back to about 10-12% a year from 2027, not 15-20%.
  - If it stays at mid-single digits, Merchant grows about 1-2% and the group misses 4-6% (e).
  - Each 5 points of Clover growth is about US$165m of revenue (2025 base US$3.3bn) and about US$0.12 EPS at a 50% incremental margin (e).
  - Plays out 2027-28.
- **Updates:** leg 1. Candidate question: "Is Clover losing share, or digesting a pull-forward?"
- **Confidence:** low on which it is. Confirmed by Q4 Clover revenue at +10% or more and a 2027 Clover guide close to GPV growth. Reversed by Q4 below +5% with GPV above +10%.

**B. Banking: fewer losses, still shrinking.**
- **What changed:**
  - Attrition −17% by count and revenue.
  - Wins +50% from a low base, skewed to larger banks.
  - Finxact past 50m accounts.
  - Banking −5% organic; core accounts −2%.
- **Puts:**
  - Better service: incidents −75%.
  - Industry demand is strong: Jack Henry cites 81% of banks raising tech budgets, and calls bank M&A neutral.
  - FIS is slipping (FY26 banking guide cut from 5-5.5% to 3.5-4%), which opens room at large banks.
- **Takes:**
  - Revenue lags wins by 9-12 months.
  - Jack Henry won 9 competitive cores in the quarter (4 a year ago), from an unnamed main rival.
  - Pricing was not answered; winning back trust may cost price.
- **Net effect:** base case FS growth of about 2% in 2027 and 3% in 2028-29 (e). Banking moving from −5% to 0% is worth about US$115m revenue and about US$0.15 EPS (e).
- **Updates:** leg 1.
- **Confidence:** medium that attrition has peaked; low on the timing of growth.

**C. Cost base reset (Project Elevate).**
- **What changed:**
  - US$1bn of savings, up from US$500m: retire overlapping platforms, exit more than a dozen small products, embed AI.
  - Headcount −3,000.
  - Non-core sales planned.
- **Puts:** US$1bn is about 5% of 2026 revenue, enough to close most of the gap from 31% to the 37% target.
- **Takes:**
  - Savings come mostly after 2027 and are back-end weighted.
  - Cost to achieve about 1:1, excluded from adjusted EPS (about US$650m a year pre-tax run-rate in 2026 (e)).
  - Reinvestment unspecified.
  - Platform migrations carry churn risk.
  - Sales shrink revenue.
- **Net effect:** base case margin 35% in 2029 (management: above 37%). 60% of US$1bn reaching profit by 2029 is about US$1.0 EPS (e).
- **Updates:** leg 2.
- **Confidence:** medium on the mechanism, low on the timing.

**D. Capital allocation.**
- **What changed:** buybacks resumed at 3.1x leverage; disposal proceeds go to buybacks and debt.
- **Puts:** at 8.6x 2026 EPS the earnings yield is about 11.6%, against an after-tax debt cost of about 4%. Buybacks add value at this price.
- **Takes:**
  - Moody's (Baa2, Nov 2025) put the downgrade line at about 3.5x on its own measure, roughly 3x reported.
  - In 2025 the company bought US$5.6bn of stock at an average of about US$174, about 2.8x today's price.
- **Updates:** leg 3; capital-allocation judgement on management.
- **Confidence:** high on accretion at this price, low on discipline through a cycle.

**E. New management (since mid-2026).**
- CEO Takis Georgakopoulos, ex-JPMorgan, previously co-president of Merchant. CFO Paul Todd, ex-Global Payments.
- Pay and ownership not reviewed. A press headline reports the CFO bought about US$0.5m of stock in June 2026; Form 4 not checked.

**Expected by now but not shown:**
- The H2 margin of 35-36% from the Feb guide (actual 31.2%).
- Clover's 15-20% revenue growth (actual +1%).

## 4. The cycle

Fiserv is not cyclical in a way that drives this thesis: about 84% of revenue is recurring, and the problems are company-specific. The outside factors that do touch it are below; these are the indicators to carry forward.

| Clover GPV, ex-gateway | +11% | +11% | +8% (reported) | Steady |

**Peers** (quarters to Sept 2026):
- **FIS (Q3):** banking +3.9% pro forma; FY guide cut; early 2027 banking flat to +1.5%; stock about −20%; CFO leaving. Agrees that big-bank conversions are slow.
- **Jack Henry (FQ1 27):** revenue +7.2% non-GAAP; guide raised; 9 competitive core wins. Differs: community-bank demand is strong.
- **Global Payments (Q3):** SMB +5% normalized; Genius bookings more than doubled. Differs.
- **Toast (Q3):** GPV +20%; take rate up; guide raised. Differs.
- **Visa (FQ4):** US volume +10%. Differs.

Read: demand is fine and Fiserv is under-earning for its own reasons.

**Earnings against normal:** under-earning. The adjusted margin of 31% compares with 37-38% in 2024-25 and the 37% target. On plain cash basis, earnings are lower still while transformation spend runs.

**What the price assumes:** see section 6. Partial recovery: roughly the conservative case at a 9x exit.

**Next period:**
- Confirms recovery: Q4 Merchant +6-8% and Clover back to double digits; FS about +2% organic; a 2027 guide with margin up.
- Means the read is wrong: Merchant below +3% with Visa still about +10% (share loss); FS negative.
