## 1. Reads (newest first)

### 2026-11-28 - first read

All of this section is inference from the event log, apart from the dated readings under Sentiment.

**Focus.** Two variables carry the price.
1. *Whether the annual guide survives the next quarter.* The five largest results-day moves all turn on it: the reset (E4), a guide kept after a feared cut (E7, E27), a guide cut (E19), and a kept guide that the market did not believe (E13).
2. *Revenue growth, not earnings per share.* A 13% EPS beat with a US$50m revenue miss fell 8.8% (E13). In 2025 the commentary on all three falls was Clover volume growth (E1 to E3, prices unverified).

A third variable showed once: management stability. The CEO's departure with guidance reaffirmed cost 10.9% in a day while peers were flat (E16).

**What it ignores.**
- EPS beats on their own (E13).
- Activist pressure. The Jana stake lifted the stock 6.9% and the gain was gone within five days (E9). The push for asset sales moved it 2.5% (E15). The letter asking for US$1.25bn of savings moved it −1.0% (E24).
- The May 2026 Investor Day targets for 2029, including EPS above US$12: +2.1% on the day, and one write-up called them "little surprise" (E14).
- A director's purchase in August (about +1%, inside E20).
- The US$6.45bn goodwill impairment and the GAAP loss on 3 Nov 2026 (E27).
- At the close, the August guide cut itself (E19); see Sentiment.

**Earnings or discount.** Mostly earnings, with a trust discount on top.
- The stock moves on results days and on guidance, which is the earnings pattern. Peer and sector days explain most of the rest (E6, E8, E11, E12, E18, E20, E21).
- Split of the last year, on fiscal-2026 estimates because forward 12-month figures were not available: the price is up 1.1% over 52 weeks (StockAnalysis, 2026-11-27). The 2026 consensus EPS fell from US$8.19 (Benzinga, 2026-02-10) to US$7.26 (TIKR, 2026-11-09), about −11% since February. So the multiple on 2026 earnings rose from about 7.3x (US$60.14, 2026-02-09) to about 8.5x (US$61.49, 2026-11-27).
- The discount part: the forward multiple was 7.1x on 2026-11-02 against a five-year average of 15x (TIKR). Commentary ties this to guidance that keeps being cut; the Jana letter's phrase, as reported, was "serial mis-forecasting" (E24).
- One recent row is a discount-type move: −6% on 19 Nov when an analyst called a debit-network sale unlikely (E29).

**The story.** The market's working story appears to be that Fiserv is a turnaround whose numbers cannot yet be trusted. The price follows whether each guide holds. The third quarter of 2026 was the first print in a year where the guide held, cost savings were doubled and buybacks returned, and the stock had its best day since 2004. The doubt that remains is growth in Merchant and Clover. Confidence: medium.

Two readings that would show the story has changed:
1. The stock falls on a results day where the guide is kept or raised, with commentary on Clover or Merchant growth. The focus would have moved from "does the guide hold" to "is the growth real".
2. The stock moves more on asset-sale, buyback or leverage news than on results, with estimates flat. The argument would have moved from earnings to the discount. E29 is one row of this kind; a pattern needs three.

**Sentiment position: Sceptical.** No earlier state is on file. The log reads as Washed out from about August to the Q3 print on 3 Nov 2026.

*Reaction asymmetry.*
- Through June 2026 the stock fell harder on bad news than it rose on good: −8.8% on a quarter with an EPS beat (E13), −10.9% on the CEO change (E16), against +4.1% (E7) and +2.1% (E14) on days that went its way.
- In August it stopped falling on bad news. An 11% cut to the EPS guide closed flat after a 12.75% fall before the open (E19). That is the washed-out pattern.
- In November modest good news produced about +12.5% in a day and about +26% in a week (E27). On 19 Nov one analyst's comment took off 6% (E29).
- Read: sentiment has lifted from Washed out, but the multiple, the rating mix and the 19 Nov fall say Sceptical, not Neutral.

5. **Not explained:** the sector moves in E11, E12 and E18. They may be sector news this run did not look for.
6. **Thresholds.** Fiserv is above US$20bn of market value (US$32.7bn, StockAnalysis, 2026-11-27), so the halved thresholds apply (2.5% a day, 5% over five days against the index). More days qualify than the log holds. The log takes results and guidance days, every day of 4% or more with a table to check it, and the dated company news; smaller days were left out.
