## Re-check 2026-11-27 (against company results and disclosures)

**Verdict: Qualifies, provisional** (proposed; unchanged). Autos are cyclical, so normal profit is capped at the ten-year average margin times current revenue. On that basis the conservative return is about 15.5% with no cash credited, and about 19.4% with cash credited at the payout rate. The earnings base is the deciding question: whether margins of 7-12% since 2021 are a new level, after 1-4% from 2017 to 2020.

**1. One-page read.** Korea's second car maker, 36.67% owned by Hyundai Motor and affiliates [S1].
- Q3 2026: revenue ₩31.6tn, +10.1%; operating profit ₩1.82tn, +24.3%; net profit ₩1.29tn [S2].
- Operating profit was 25.5% below consensus, because of raw-material costs and incentives to sell electric cars [S3].
- US hybrids are now 29.3% of US sales [S4].
- Downturns: operating profit was about ₩0.66tn in 2017 (the THAAD dispute) [S5], and the margin was 3.5% in 2019-20 [S6].

**2. Zone and five tests.**
- Price: ₩95,800 on 2026-11-11 [S7]; ₩100,300 at the close on 6 Nov, when the market cap was ₩39.16tn [S8]. On 390.4m shares, about ₩37.4tn.
- Net cash: ₩21.3tn (Capital IQ, first check). Kia has no captive finance arm.
- Operating margin by year: 2017 1.2%, 2018 2.1%, 2019 3.5%, 2020 3.5%, 2021 7.3%, 2022 8.4%, 2023 11.6%, 2024 11.8%, 2025 about 7.95% [S6][S9]; 2016 about 4.7% (estimated). Ten-year average about 6.2%.
- Normal net profit: 6.2% times revenue of about ₩120-123tn, times a net-to-operating ratio of 0.78: **about ₩5.8-6.0tn**.

**3. Governance and cash: Unclear.** The cash may be called on for group investment (US and European plants, electric-car platforms). The size of Kia's group commitments was not read.

**4. Why cheap, and who is selling.** The Q3 miss, tariffs, the war and electric-car price competition. The stock fell 11.7% on the day of the results [S8], from a 52-week high of ₩172,100 [S1]. NH and Samsung Securities cut their targets on 2 Nov [S7].

**5. Carry.** About 7% from the dividend, plus any buyback within the 35% TSR.

**6. Harsh case.** At 2020 profit, 4-5% a year. That is a heavy loss of return if margins return to the old regime: the dividend would then rest on the cash.
