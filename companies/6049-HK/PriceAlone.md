# Poly Property Services (6049-HK) — price-alone check, 2026-11-15

**Verdict:** Qualifies, provisional

## 1. One-page read
Chinese property-services discount; parent developer's profits falling.

## 2. Zone and five tests
Earnings base Pass (profit RMB1.38bn 2023 to RMB1.55bn 2025); trade receivables RMB3.4bn, 89% under a year. Five tests overall: **Pass**.

## 3. Governance and cash
State-owned Poly group controls it. Cash and bank balances RMB12.9bn (Dec 2025). Payout 50%. Parent Poly Developments' H1 2026 profit fell 41%. Governance and cash: **Unclear**.

## 4. Why cheap, who is selling
Chinese property-services discount; parent developer's profits falling.

## 5. Carry and triggers
Carry (dividend plus buyback yield, approx.): 5.3%. Triggers are upside only.

## 6. Cautious and harsh cases (at the Capital IQ screen price, Nov 2026)
- Market cap: 12,272 CNYm; net cash 12,886 CNYm credited at 50%.
- Normal profit 1,550.0 CNYm (FY2025 net profit) → cautious return **26.6%** (no growth, no re-rating).
- Trough profit 1,380.0 → harsh return **23.7%**. Loss at the 10-year-low multiple not yet computed.

## 7. Verdict and open items
Qualifies, provisional. Open items: Desk: how much of the cash is owners' funds; room to raise the payout.

Exit conditions: (a) cautious return at the then-current price below 15%; (b) any test or governance turns to Fail; (c) after 2-3 years, carry received well short of the above.

## Re-check 2026-11-27 (against company results and disclosures)

**Verdict: Qualifies, provisional** (proposed; unchanged). The conservative return falls from 26.6% to about 17%. The first check used FY2025 profit and credited half the cash. This one uses the three-year average, and credits only the cash left after all liabilities, because a property manager holds owners' prepaid fees.

**1. One-page read.** The property-services arm of state-owned Poly Developments.
- Revenue: RMB 17.13bn in 2025, +4.8%; attributable profit RMB 1.55bn, +5.1% [S1].
- Property management revenue grew 12.6% in 2025 to RMB 13.15bn, 76.7% of the total [S2].
- Third-party projects now give 46.5% of management fees. The two developer-linked lines are shrinking: non-owner services -16.7% and community services -14.4% in H1 2026 [S3].
- No downturn yet in profit: H1 2026 attributable profit RMB 933m, +4.8% [S3].

**2. Zone and five tests.**
- Price: HK$25.72 on 2026-11-22 [S4]. 553.33m shares, so about HK$14.2bn, or about RMB 13.1bn at 0.92 (Claude's conversion).
- Cash and bank balances: RMB 12.34bn at June 2026, against RMB 12.89bn at December 2025 [S5]. No interest-bearing debt [S2].
- Normal profit: RMB 1.47bn, the 2023-25 average. 2021-22 were not read.

**3. Governance and cash: Unclear.**
- Poly Developments holds 68.81% [S4]. Its attributable profit fell 79% in 2025 [S8] and 41% in H1 2026 [S9].
- The risk is the parent: receivables from its projects, any deposits with group entities, and pressure for related-party work.
- Trade receivables were RMB 3.44bn, 89% under a year [S6]. How much is owed by the Poly group was not read.

**4. Why cheap.** The Chinese property-services discount, with a weakening parent.

**5. Carry.** About 5.9% from the dividend. A trigger would be a higher payout or an interim dividend; upside only.

**6. Harsh case.** About 16% at 2023 profit, crediting cash net of all liabilities.

**7. Open items.**
- *Desk:* the 2025 annual report on HKEX: receivables from the Poly group; deposits or cash with group entities; related-party revenue and its caps.
- *Wait:* 2026 results, March 2027: H2 operating cash flow and the payout.
