# SANKYO (6417.T) — Post-primer triage, 2026-11-27

**Verdict: Dig, narrow** (proposed; awaiting Paul's confirmation). The plan is a frame first, which will most likely route the name to the price-alone check, not to the full debate.

## 1. Business quality: Marginal

- **Returns: Pass on the last four years, weak before them.** ROE was 16-20% from FY3/2023 to FY3/2026 with no debt and cash at two-thirds of assets, but 2-7% from FY3/2020 to FY3/2022 [Primer 4.1]. Operating margin swings from 11% to 38% with volume, because R&D of over ¥20bn a year is fixed [Primer 4.1].
- **Durability: Pass.** More than 30% of pachinko units for four years. The runner-up sells about a third of SANKYO's volume, and a captive base of SANKYO cabinets takes its cheaper replacement boards (64% of H1 pachinko units) [Primer 1.1, 3.2].
- **Runway: Fail.** The pachinko installed base fell from 2.34m to 1.87m machines between 2021 and 2025, and halls close at about 4% a year. Pachislot is flat at about 1.36m [Primer 3.1]. Sales of ¥179bn in FY3/2026 sit between the ¥58-85bn of FY3/2020-22 and the ¥199bn peak, which came from a forced replacement wave [Primer 4.1].

A good business in a shrinking market. That rules out the compounding thesis and leaves the cash return as the case.

## 2. Edge

The gate hypothesis (2026-11-15) was duration and framing: priced as a dying maker, though it leads pachinko and its payout floor gives about a 5% yield. The primer changes it to a **discount-leg, price-alone** claim.

- The market caps the stock at about ¥435bn on 198.8m outstanding shares (230m issued less 31.2m in treasury; Claude's arithmetic). Net cash is about ¥190bn [Primer 4.2], about 44% of that.
- Ex-cash, the price is about 6.4 times the company's FY3/2027 net profit plan of ¥40bn (Claude's arithmetic).
- Cash does reach holders. From FY3/2024 to FY3/2026 the company paid out ¥212bn against net profit of ¥155bn, and the dividend floor is now a 5% DOE [Primer 4.2, 4.3].
- The open question is the earnings base. Net profit was ¥6-19bn a year in FY3/2020-22, ¥47-54bn in FY3/2023-26, and ¥40bn is planned this year. The market may be pricing the last four years as the peak of a replacement wave.
- **Type:** price alone, with a Probability candidate (the fade is slower than priced). **Leg:** discount.
- **Coverage:** 4 analysts in one listing, 12 at the gate. The consensus target of about ¥1,974 sits below the price [S1].
- **Likely seller:** funds that exclude gambling (the gate's hypothesis; not evidenced yet), and holders who see a shrinking market.

## 3. Timing

The price is ¥2,189.5 at the close on 9 Nov 2026 [S1], and about ¥2,230 around 12 Nov [S2]. Neither is today's price. On those prices:

| Earnings base (net profit) | Ex-cash earnings yield | With a 2% yearly decline |
|---|---|---|
| ¥34bn, the 7-year average FY3/2020-26 | about 13% | **about 11%** |
| ¥40bn, the FY3/2027 plan | about 15.6% | **about 13.6%** |

Cash is credited in full, because the record shows it is paid out. This is short of the 15% hurdle but within two to four points of it, so the value-trap check applies. A smaller decline or a continued buyback closes the gap without any re-rating. Price decides only whether to dig now; the gap is small enough to dig.

## 4. Learning value

Low. No other name in the pipeline is in this industry. The primer is enough reference on how Japan's pachinko industry works.
