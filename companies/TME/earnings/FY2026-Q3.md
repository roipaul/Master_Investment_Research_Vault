# Tencent Music Entertainment (TME) - FY2026 Q3 earnings update (reported 2026-11-17)

Gaps:
- **No slide deck.** TME does not publish one.
- **Call read from secondary sources.** Read from a third-party transcript (Singju Post) and two AI-assisted call summaries (Investing.com, Yahoo Finance), not the S&P/IR transcript. Upload the S&P transcript to verify quotes.
- **Release only.** The HKEX quarterly announcement was not read separately.
- **No Ximalaya split.** TME did not disclose Ximalaya's revenue for Q3. Every organic figure below is derived and marked (e).
- **Consensus.** Figures come from press reports and are context only.
- **No baseline documents.** There is no `Thesis.md`, debate, variant view or triage on file. This run uses the provisional baseline below, built from the Nov 2026 [[Primer]] and the value DCF model (`files/`, v1 Nov 8, v2 Nov 17).

**Provisional baseline.** This is Claude's framing. Paul has stated strong interest in TME after its fall and asked about the Soda threat, but he has not stated a view.

The return depends on three things:
1. The paid core holds, meaning organic membership stays flat or better while Soda grows, because SVIP pricing offsets lost casual payers.
2. Core EBITA margin stays near 28-30% as the mix moves to concerts and merchandise.
3. Net cash and the listed stakes, about US$4.6 per ADS, are returned rather than spent on low-return deals.

What to track: organic membership growth (derived), year-end paying users (now disclosed only annually), adjusted EBITDA and core EBITA margin, marketing & consumption growth, Soda MAU (QuestMobile), buybacks and dividends, and M&A.

What would break it:
- Organic membership down more than 3% YoY for two quarters, or year-end paying users below 120m (127.4m at Q4 2025).
- Core EBITA margin below 26% for a full year.
- Another large acquisition at a low return, or a halt to buybacks.

Missing: triage, debate with probabilities, variant view. On the question "continued core growth against Soda", Claude's earlier read was **low confidence** (chat, Sept-Nov 2026).

**Correction to the Nov 17 chat note.** TME stopped quarterly reporting of MAU, paying users and ARPPU from Q1 2026, as announced with the Q4 2025 results on 2026-03-17. Q3 2026 is not the first quarter without them, and Q2 did not report about 128m paying users. That figure was a data-provider estimate. The last reported count is 127.4m at Q4 2025. The re-segmentation into membership and marketing & consumption also dates from Q1 2026.

## 1. Bottom line

**Thesis: Weakened (provisional baseline). Organic membership slipped to about −1% YoY (e) after +7% in Q1 and +3% (e) in Q2, and organic revenue fell about 5% (e). Cash generation and buybacks held up.** (Claude's read)

**Fresh money at US$9.45 (2026-11-17): Wait.** The base case returns about 19% a year and the harsh case loses about 9%. But the thesis has weakened, and the open question can be settled by FY2026 results in about mid-March 2027. That question is whether organic membership has stabilised or the paid core is starting to shrink. Two settling readings: Q4 organic membership flat or better, and year-end paying users of 125m or more. On price, the conservative case clears 15% a year at about US$8.10 or below. (Claude's read)

## 3. Structural changes: puts and takes

**A. The paid core has stopped growing organically.**
- **What changed:** organic membership c.RMB 4,535m (e), −0.6% YoY. Membership on the new basis was 4,284 / 4,434 / 4,564 / 4,625 in 2025 (Q1 2026 6-K). It was 4,570 in Q1 2026, which management linked to Kugou free-user competition.
- **Puts:** SVIP still grows (claim). Price tiers let TME lift revenue per payer while losing casual payers. Prepaid memberships keep cash conversion high.
- **Takes:** the casual payer base is exposed to free Soda and Kugou/Free Music cannibalisation. Membership has the highest incremental margin, so each lost 1% costs about RMB 180m of revenue and about RMB 150m of core EBITA (e).
- **Net effect:** model v2 assumes paying users flat at about 127-128m and ARPPU +1.2% a year, so organic membership grows about 1% a year. A −2% a year path instead (the conservative case) lowers 2030E NOPAT by about RMB 1.9bn (e). Plays out 2027-2029.
- **Updates:** leg 1 of the baseline. In the primer's terms, it answers "is Soda taking paid users?" with probably yes at the margin.
- **Confidence:** medium. Confirmed by year-end paying users below 125m or Q4 organic membership down again. Reversed by paying users of 127m or more with flat Q4 organic membership.

**B. Growth moves to IP, merchandise and concerts.**
- **What changed:** marketing & consumption +12% reported, led by merchandise (Silence Wang) and stadium shows. Gross margin 42.8% vs 43.5%. Management says IP margin is below group average but improving.
- **Puts:** fan monetisation that Soda cannot copy quickly. It feeds SVIP bundles (ticket priority, merchandise), and Q4 should accelerate (claim).
- **Takes:** lower margin, hit-driven artist calendars, an "intense" concert market and more working capital. It does not offset the organic ad decline: c.−5% organic (e).
- **Net effect:** a 1-point lower group gross margin is about RMB 360m of EBITA a year (e). Model v2 holds core EBITA margin at about 29% from 2027.
- **Updates:** leg 2.
- **Confidence:** medium-high on the mix effect.

**C. Ximalaya: growth bought, at a low return.**
- **What changed:** c.RMB 14bn consideration (MBW, citing the interim accounts). Run-rate revenue c.RMB 3.5bn (e). Acquisition amortisation of about RMB 0.58bn a year.
- **Puts:** long-form audio in SVIP; ads; consolidation of the audio market. Management describes it as past its hardest competitive period.
- **Takes:** ByteDance's free Fanqie Changting. If Ximalaya earns a 10-15% EBITA margin (e), NOPAT is about RMB 0.3-0.45bn, or 2-3% on RMB 14bn. That is about RMB 9-10bn of value given up versus returning the cash (e), about US$0.9 per ADS. The money is spent, so the point is capital allocation trust.
- **Updates:** leg 3 (cash returned, not spent).
- **Confidence:** medium. The margin is undisclosed.

**D. The free-tier response.** TME Free Music launched on 2026-09-10 (ad-funded, swipe-to-listen). There is also a Kugou free version and a Weixin Channels funnel.
- **Puts:** it defends traffic and the ad base.
- **Takes:** it can cannibalise paid tiers at lower revenue per user.
- **Net:** not sizable yet. Watch whether the year-end paying-user count is defined to include the new tiers.

**Expected but not seen:** organic membership growth from SVIP upgrades (the Q2 hope), and a new buyback programme.

## 4. The cycle

TME is not cyclical in a way that matters to the thesis: the driver is competitive, ByteDance's free model taking traffic. Three outside factors touch it: the China advertising market (pressure, cited by management), consumer spending on concerts and merchandise (strong in Q3), and RMB/US$. The RMB has strengthened to about 6.70 from 7.1 in 2025 (release convenience rate), which lifts the US$ value of RMB earnings.

Competitive indicators, carried forward each quarter:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Organic membership YoY | c.−0.6% (e) | c.+3.3% (e) | n.a. (new basis) | Worse |
| Marketing & consumption, organic YoY | c.−5% (e) | c.+8% (e) | n.a. | Worse |
| Social entertainment YoY | −15.7% | −16.4% | - | Flat (structural decline) |
| Gross margin | 42.8% | 44.2% | 43.5% | Worse |
| Adjusted EBITDA margin | 33.8% | 36.4% | 34.8% | Worse |
| Soda MAU (QuestMobile) | 167m, Jun 2026, +68.7% (latest found) | 156.1m, Mar 2026 | 120.4m, Sept 2025 | Up |

**Peers** (periods as stated):
- **Spotify Q3 2026** (2026-10-22): subscribers 306m (+6m net adds), premium ARPU +7.5% at constant currency, gross margin 33.0%. Global paid streaming is healthy and pricing works, so TME's problem is China-specific competition, not the industry.
- **NetEase Cloud Music H1 2026** (Aug 2026): revenue +3.4%, membership +4%, operating profit −11% on promotion. The incumbent in second place is also slowing.
- **Soda:** still growing fast as of June. No QuestMobile reading after June 2026 was found. A Morningstar note title says Soda is pulling back on freebies; the text was not reached, so this is unverified.

**Phase:** not applicable. Competitive position: the paid core is under slow erosion; the SVIP tier is described as stable (claim). Earnings against mid-cycle: not applicable. The question is the level of the paid base, not a cycle.

**What the price assumes:** at US$9.45 the core business is valued at about 6.0x 2027E NOPAT, which equals free cash flow falling about 7% a year forever at a 10% discount rate (computed, section 6).

**Next period:** Q4 organic membership and the year-end paying-user count settle the question. Q4 marketing & consumption should accelerate if the IP claim holds.

## 6. Valuation and the fresh-money view

**Inputs**
- **Price:** US$9.45 (2026-11-17 intraday).
- **Diluted ADS:** 1,560.8m. Derived from the Q1 2026 weighted basic count plus 175.3m Ximalaya shares, less the Q2-Q3 buybacks of 156.4m shares, plus 10.7m dilutive ADS.
- **Net cash:** RMB 29.7bn (2026-09-30), US$2.84 per ADS.
- **Non-core value after haircuts:** US$4.56 per ADS. Calculation: net cash US$2.84, plus stakes after a 20% haircut, equity-method and other investments US$2.19, less a 10% withholding haircut on the 80% of cash assumed onshore (US$0.39), less debt-like items (US$0.07).

**Scenarios.** Value per ADS at end-2029 equals core value (2030E NOPAT × exit multiple, less 2.7% minority interest), plus Q4 2026-2029 free cash flow to holders, plus today's non-core value. The hold is 3.1 years. Interest on cash is ignored, and buybacks are treated as neutral.

| Case | 2030E revenue / core margin | 2030E NOPAT | Exit multiple | Core value | FCF Q4 26-29 | Non-core | Total | Return a year |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base (model v2) | RMB 39.2bn / 29.3% | RMB 9.47bn | 10x | US$8.81 | US$2.72 | US$4.56 | US$16.09 | **+18.6%** |
| Conservative: organic membership −2% a year, M&C +4%, social −10%, margin to 26% | RMB 35.0bn / 26% | RMB 7.52bn | 8x | US$5.59 | US$2.38 | US$4.56 | US$12.54 | **+9.5%** |
| Harsh: membership −6% a year, M&C flat, social −15%, margin to 20%, stakes −30% | RMB 29.7bn / 20% | RMB 4.91bn | 6x | US$2.74 | US$1.88 | US$4.01 | US$8.63 | **−2.9%** (−9% total) |

**What the price implies:**
- Market core value = (US$9.45 − US$4.56) × 1,560.8m × 6.70 ÷ 0.973 ≈ RMB 52.6bn. That is 6.0x 2027E NOPAT (RMB 8.8bn).
- Perpetual free-cash-flow growth implied at a 10% discount rate is about −7% a year.
- The conservative case returns about the cost of equity, so **today's price implies roughly the conservative case:** a slow decline in the paid core and margins easing to about 26%.

**Price move:**
- The ADS fell about 7% from US$9.56 (Nov 6) to US$8.88 into the print, was down 2.4% premarket, then rose about 6% after the call to about US$9.45.
- The market took the print as "membership in line, IP strong, buyback intact". Jefferies raised its target to US$13.30 (Buy).
- It did not price the organic decline because consensus measures membership with Ximalaya included.

**Fresh-money view.** Someone meeting TME today gets US$4.56 per ADS of cash and stakes, plus a core business priced at about 6x after-tax operating profit that throws off about US$0.55 per ADS a year in free cash flow (2027E) and returns most of it.

What has to be true is that the paid core shrinks slowly or not at all. The evidence on file points to slow erosion:
- Organic membership flat-to-down for four quarters.
- Soda MAU +69% to June.
- Advertising losing traffic.
- SVIP described as stable but unquantified.

The downside is protected by cash, stakes and capital return: the harsh case loses about 9% over three years. The base case clears the hurdle. The value-trap check is not failed, given buybacks of about 2% of ADSs a quarter, but it is a live risk: Ximalaya shows cash can go into low-return deals.

The rules allow Own only on an Intact or Strengthened thesis, and this print weakened it. So the call is **Wait**:
- **Evidence:** FY2026 results (about mid-March 2027) with the year-end paying-user count and Q4 membership. Own if paying users are 125m or more and Q4 organic membership is flat or better. Don't own if paying users are below 120m or organic membership falls more than 3%.
- **Price:** at about US$8.10 or below, the conservative case clears 15% a year (computed: US$12.54 ÷ 1.15^3.12). That is an entry level from the scenario table, not a target.

**Model rebase:** not due. Rebased to v2 on 2026-11-17 with Q3 actuals and the Sept 30 balance sheet. Refresh the Spotify and UMG prices, which are still at Nov 6 levels.
