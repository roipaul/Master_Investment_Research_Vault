## 1. Reads (newest first)

### 2026-11-28 - first read

All of this section is inference from the event log, apart from the dated readings under Sentiment.

**Focus.** One variable carries the price: the health of the paying base.
- Each large results-day fall came with commentary on subscription or membership growth slowing, not on profit: −8.4% on a quarter that beat (E1), −24.7% when subscription growth slowed to 13% and the company stopped reporting users and ARPPU (E5), −11.9% when membership growth came in "slightly over 8%" (E12).
- Behind it, every piece of commentary names the same two causes: ByteDance's Soda (Qishui) Music and AI-made music.
- The disclosure itself became part of the focus in March. With no quarterly user counts, membership revenue growth is the only reading the market gets.

**What it ignores.**
- Beats. Revenue and earnings ahead of consensus fell 8.4% in November 2025 (E1); a revenue beat on one consensus fell 11.9% in August 2026 (E12).
- Buybacks. About US$300m repurchased in the third quarter, a completed programme and "another round" prepared in August, and a US$400m programme reported in September did not hold the price (E12; E13 is the one possible exception and its date is uncertain).
- Headline revenue growth. It now comes from the Ximalaya acquisition and from concerts and merchandise.

**Earnings or discount.** The trigger is earnings news; the adjustment has been in the multiple.
- The stock moves on results days, which is the earnings pattern.
- But near-term estimates have moved little. Consensus 2026 EPS is RMB 3.08, 3.7% above 2025, and 2027 is RMB 3.30 (StockAnalysis, estimates as of 2026-08-17).
- The multiple went from 19x FY26 earnings (a Seeking Alpha author's figure, 2025-11-13) to 11.5x trailing (Motley Fool, 2026-03-20) and 9.0x forward (StockAnalysis, 2026-10-16). Over about the same span the price fell from US$18.68 (2025-11-13) to US$8.63 (2026-10-16), −54%. On this arithmetic almost the whole fall is the multiple.
- Read: the market is not arguing about next year's profit. It is arguing about how long the paying base lasts, and it has answered by paying half as much for each yuan of earnings.

**The story.** The market's working story appears to be that Tencent Music's paid core is being eroded by a free rival and by AI-made music, that management stopped reporting users because the numbers were getting worse, and that profit, buybacks and acquired revenue do not answer that. The third quarter of 2026 was the first results day in five to rise, on membership revenue growth of 9.2%. Confidence: medium.

Two readings that would show the story has changed:
1. A results day where membership growth disappoints and the stock does not fall. The erosion would then be in the price.
2. The stock starting to move on capital return or on the value of its stakes, with membership news no longer moving it. The argument would have moved from the paying base to the discount on the assets.

**Sentiment position: Sceptical.** No earlier state is on file. The log reads as Washed out from about mid-September to the Q3 print on 17 Nov 2026.

*Reaction asymmetry.*
- For four results days running the stock fell whatever the numbers were (E1, E5, E9, E12).
- On 17 Nov 2026 revenue missed one consensus by 2.2%, the stock was down 3.1% before the open, and it closed up about 6.6% (E16). That is the first day in the log where it stopped falling on mixed news.
- It has risen further since, to US$10.10 (E17), with no news read.
- Read: the washed-out pattern showed once and the price has since lifted a third from the low. One row is not a pattern, the multiple is still about half of a year ago, and four in ten analysts are at hold. Sceptical, not Neutral.

**Not passed on.** Sceptical is not an extreme, so no flag goes to the hub. For reference only: the price is above the hub's reference entry levels of about US$8.10 to US$8.30 (Claude's, set 2026-11-24). The closes from 29 Sep to 8 Oct 2026 were below them, before they were set.
