# Yunnan Aluminium (000807.SZ) — Post-primer triage, 2026-11-27

**Verdict: Defer** (proposed; awaiting Paul's confirmation).

## 1. Business quality: Marginal

- **Returns: Pass for 2023-25.** Weighted ROE was 16.5-19.7% in 2023-25 and 21.9% for H1 2026. Net cash was about RMB 21bn at Q3 2026 [Primer 4.2, secondary].
- **Durability: Marginal.** Hydropower is the cost advantage and the carbon advantage, but the company buys that power from the grid rather than owning it. It is cut in dry seasons: by more than 40% in early 2023 [Primer 1.1]. It also buys about three-quarters of its alumina, most of it from Chalco [Primer 1.1].
- **Runway: Fail.** Volume sits at the national cap. The 2026 target of 3.19 Mt is below 2025's output [Primer 4.1]. All earnings variance is price.

## 2. Edge

The gate hypothesis (2026-11-15) was duration: 5.7 times 2026 earnings prices a peak, while the 45 Mt cap is binding. Since the primer it is **weaker**:
- The aluminium cycle note (2026-11-26) reads the cycle as *Rolling over*. It finds output and capacity counts above the cap, which questions how binding the cap is [[aluminium]].
- The stock has already fallen from its 52-week high of RMB 38.23 to about RMB 23-25 [S1][S2]. The market is pricing a fade, and the cycle note's indicators have weakened since Q3.
- **Type:** duration. **Leg:** earnings.
- **Coverage:** 18 analysts at the gate.
- **Likely seller:** holders rotating out of a cyclical at peak margins. No forced seller.

## 3. Timing

At RMB 23.21-24.72 (early to mid-November 2026, not today's price) [S1][S2], the market cap is RMB 80.5-85.7bn on 3,468m shares. The price is about 5.2-5.5 times 2026 earnings run at nine-month pace (RMB 15.5bn).

The cycle note puts mid-cycle profit at about RMB 6.4bn on a three-year base, which may be too low. On that figure, with all the net cash credited, the earnings yield is about 10-11%, with no growth. That is short of the hurdle while the cycle is rolling over.

Reference levels (Claude's arithmetic, not a buy price): the conservative case clears 15% at about RMB 18.4 if mid-cycle is RMB 6.4bn, or about RMB 21.4 if it is RMB 8bn.

## 4. Learning value

Medium. It is the Chinese half of the aluminium spread that Alcoa sits on the other side of, and both feed one cycle note. That overlap is why the gate let it in; it does not make it a Dig.

## 5. Defer

**Trigger.** Any one of these:
- the price falls to about RMB 18-21, the range where the conservative case clears 15%;
- the aluminium cycle note moves to Downturn or Trough;
- ten-year margins show mid-cycle profit well above RMB 6.4bn.

**Revisit:** April 2027, after the 2026 annual report.

**Signals to track until then:**
1. The SHFE aluminium price against domestic alumina, and the smelting spread in the cycle note.
2. Any Yunnan dry-season power curtailment order (November to May).
3. The 2026 final dividend and the payout ratio, against 40% in 2025 [Primer 4.3].
4. Where the cash is held: the balance with Chinalco Finance against its RMB 9bn cap.
5. China's operating capacity against the 45 Mt cap.

**View to re-read at the revisit:** A capped, hydro-powered smelter earning about two and a half times its recent average because alumina is cheap and aluminium is firm. The cash and the dividend are real, and so is the 40% payout. But there is no volume growth, the power is borrowed, and the spread decides everything. It is worth owning near a trough, or at a price that assumes one. At present it is neither. Chalco (2600.HK) is the alternative if the view is on the spread itself.
